# Universal Ideation Facilitator

Loaded when the subject has no software surface at all — naming, narrative, personal decisions, non-digital business strategy, physical products. (Software artifacts route to elsewhere-software and never load this file, even when the ideas are about copy or visual design for them.) Phase 1 elsewhere grounding has already run; learnings-researcher was skipped. The generate-many → critique-all → explain-survivors mechanism is unchanged — only the framing adapts to the domain.

## Facilitation deltas

- Be a divergent thinking partner, not a delivery service — resist converging early; a premature favorite crowds out better candidates.
- Match tone to stakes: business decisions lead with constraints and tradeoffs; creative work leads with energy and range; personal decisions lead with values before mechanics.
- Match depth to scope: **Quick** (one round, brief critique, 3-5 survivors), **Standard** (light intake, one round, 5-7 survivors), **Full** (rich intake, parallel frames, deep critique).
- Intake follows SKILL.md 0.2/0.4 rules: subject and substance only, discrimination-tested, stop on dismissive answers, skip entirely on rich up-front context.
- **Grounding freshness:** if intake materially refines the topic (new scope, different audience, domain shift), re-dispatch the affected Phase 1 agents before generating — ranking against stale grounding surfaces ideas fit to the wrong topic.

## Generation deltas

Use SKILL.md Phase 2 as written — same six frames read domain-agnostically, same axis decomposition and spread rules (Phase 1.5 criteria), same per-idea contract, tagged-basis requirement, and generation rules. Domain adjustments:

- Non-software topics are often atomic by nature (a name, a tagline, a plot) — expect the `Decomposition skipped — atomic subject` path frequently; the candidate *is* the deliverable.
- The meeting-test maps to the domain's equivalent of "worth talking through," not literal team discussion.
- Axis-coverage recovery is extra generation batches by the facilitator, not sub-agent dispatch.

## Convergence and wrap-up

Follow `references/post-ideation-workflow.md` for critique, scoring, presentation, persistence, and the three-option menu. One delta: **Brainstorm a selected idea** is not the first step of an implementation chain — there is no `ce-plan`/`ce-work` after; `ce-brainstorm` in universal mode develops the idea (a name into a brand brief, a plot into an outline, a decision into a weighed framework) and ends there.
